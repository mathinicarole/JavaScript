My Achievements

WINNERS

I participated in the Microsoft hackathon, Game of Learners (GOL) Season 4, where I collaborated with fellow developers to create innovative technological solutions for health challenges in Africa. Our team developed JAMBOCARE, a web application that facilitates real-time communication between patients and medical professionals, overcoming language barriers. This experience has been transformative for me, as I've witnessed significant growth in my skills and perspective. Through GOL, I've deepened my knowledge of the tech industry, joined vibrant communities, become a speaker at several events, and actively engaged in organizing tech events and learning sessions. Moreover, this journey has enhanced my ability to collaborate, build relationships, and expand my network within the tech community.

Career Mentorship

Global Mentorship Initiative

In March, Microsoft launched the ADC WINS program through the Microsoft Africa Development Centre in Kenya, focusing on empowering women in STEM at local universities. The program offers mentorship through the Global Mentorship Initiative, helping bridge the gap between graduation and early career phases for underrepresented young professionals. I was matched with Joana Santos, a Data Science Lead at Microsoft, on June 3rd, 2024, and began my mentorship shortly after. This journey has been transformative, enhancing my job preparation skills, boosting my confidence, and connecting me with inspiring women who motivate me to pursue my goals and dreams.

My journey with Bitcoin began in 2022, sparked by my passion for software development.
